About the Project

Civic Jury Simulator is a citizen-participation interactive simulation platform
that allows users to experience judicial procedures through AI-powered virtual cases.

Trial Process

Follow the procedure of how a case reaches its final verdict.

Case
Creation

The administrator generates an AI-based virtual case.

Prosecution Vote

Citizens vote between proceeding with prosecution vs suspending prosecution.

Trial Commencement

The trial begins or is dismissed based on the voting results.

Evidence Submission

Users collect evidence materials and upload them to discussion threads.

Final
Argument Vote

Participants select the most convincing argument among the presented pleadings.

Final Verdict

The administrator (judge) delivers the final decision.
